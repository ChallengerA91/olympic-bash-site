The Pitch

The genre you already know how to play. A pantheon you've never fought with.

Real-time, both lanes

Two bridges, one river, three towers a side. Deploy on your half, read the push, and defend before it's too late — every match is 2:30 of live decisions, never turns.

Eight cards, one god

Build a deck around a single God or Titan — never both. Spend elixir, read the next card in your cycle, and out-think an opponent running a different pantheon entirely.

Nothing here is for sale

No card was ever stronger because you paid for it. Every stat is public, every match is skill. If we ever charge for anything, it's a look — never an edge.

Battling Friends

No server. Your computers just talk to each other.

Matches run directly between two machines — one hosts, one joins. Nothing about the fight passes through us, which means nothing about the fight can lag behind us either.
